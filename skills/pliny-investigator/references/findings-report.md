# Findings report contract

Use sections proportionate to evidence and stakes. Do not manufacture empty sections or numerical scores when the evidence cannot support them.

## Objective and decision
- Desired outcome; decision owner; proposed approach; success metric and constraints.

## Evidence ledger
| ID | Claim | Class (OBSERVED/INFERRED/HYPOTHESIS/UNKNOWN) | Source or test | Confidence/limitation |

Separate actual source findings from assumptions and from possible explanations.

## Critical assumptions
- Up to three consequential assumptions; why each matters; what would invalidate it.

## Alternatives
| Option | Mechanism | Benefits | Risks/costs | Reversibility | Supporting evidence |

Include baseline/status quo and a no-LLM/simple solution when pertinent.

## Discriminating experiment
- Hypothesis / null hypothesis.
- Inputs, baseline, change, metric, pre-registered success and failure threshold.
- Leakage/confounder checks; permission gate; exact steps if authorized.
- Interpretation of positive, negative and inconclusive results.

## Recommendation
- Decision now; evidence strength; unresolved question; next action; explicit stopping rule.

## Handoff
- Artifacts read/created, commands actually executed, tests actually run, outstanding approvals and owner.
